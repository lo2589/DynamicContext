The grid is derived, not stored. Every cell is computed on the fly from the interval written on the ledger record — there is no separate state file behind this page. Retired slots stay in the conversation, greyed: kept on record, no longer speaking. In the long run, watch the summary row: a cell goes dark and lights again later. That summary was never recomputed — a second interval was appended to it.

One honest limit. The ledger records intervals, not events: it says exactly when a slot was visible, never what caused it to stop. The compacted strip is inferred — a turn that retires a batch rather than the routine single expiry. A summary is also anchored at the turn it covers, not the later turn it was written on, so it appears earlier in the timeline than it was produced.
